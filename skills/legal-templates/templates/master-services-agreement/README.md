## Overview

A Master Services Agreement (MSA) governs ongoing commercial and technical relationships between a technology/software provider and an enterprise customer. It sets the baseline legal terms for deploying SaaS platforms, AI/ML tools, APIs, and associated professional implementation services across one or more signed Order Forms or Statements of Work (SOWs).

## When You Need This Template

Use this template when selling B2B software, cloud platform access, or technical services to commercial clients:

- Licensing a proprietary SaaS platform, AI/ML system, or API to enterprise customers
- Establishing a framework agreement under which multiple Order Forms or Statements of Work can be executed over time
- Defining ownership boundaries between customer data/inputs and provider platform/models/integration tools
- Protecting intellectual property, training weights, and algorithms while delivering outputs to clients
- Specifying standard enterprise terms for fees, invoicing, SLAs, warranties, indemnification, and liability limitations

## Why This Template

Drafted specifically for modern software companies, cloud platforms, and AI application providers. Traditional vendor MSAs often fail to address contemporary technical architectures, connected accounts, model inputs/outputs, and de-identified telemetry. Key modern features include:

- **AI & Data Architecture Ready**: Explicitly separates Customer Data and Inputs from Provider Integration Tools, Pre-built Judges, and Aggregated/De-identified Telemetry
- **Connected Accounts & API Integrations**: Clarifies rights and security boundaries when the software connects to third-party customer accounts (GitHub, AWS, Slack, CRMs)
- **Balanced Risk Allocation**: Mutual confidentiality, IP indemnity for platform infringement, standard SaaS warranty disclaimers, and clear caps on liability (e.g. fees paid over the prior 12 months)
- **Order Form & Exhibit Architecture**: Includes Exhibit A (Order Form template) and Exhibit B (Supplemental Customer Terms framework) for frictionless commercial customization

## Key Provisions

- **Platform Access & License**: Grant of non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access the cloud platform during the Order Term
- **Data Rights & IP**:
  - Customer owns all Customer Data, Inputs, and resulting Outputs
  - Provider retains all Intellectual Property Rights in the Platform, Integration Tools, and pre-built analytical components
  - Provider receives license to use Aggregated Data (de-identified, statistical telemetry) to operate and improve services
- **Connected Accounts**: Customer responsibility for third-party credentials and authorization to ingest connected account data
- **Fees & Payment**: Invoicing terms, payment windows, late payment remedies, and applicable taxes
- **Warranties & Disclaimers**: Performance warranty, exclusion of implied warranties (merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose), and as-is disclaimers
- **Indemnification**: Provider indemnifies Customer against third-party IP infringement claims; Customer indemnifies Provider against Customer Data violations
- **Limitations of Liability**: Mutual waiver of indirect, incidental, special, and consequential damages; aggregate liability cap tied to fees paid under the applicable Order
- **Term & Termination**: Order Term renewal, termination for material breach or bankruptcy, and post-termination transition assistance
