## Overview

An Advisor Agreement formalizes the relationship between a startup or company and an individual strategic advisor. It defines the advisory services, equity-based compensation (typically stock options), intellectual property assignment, confidentiality obligations, and institutional conflict rules.

## When You Need This Template

Use this agreement when engaging an advisor, industry expert, mentor, or board advisor who will provide guidance, introductions, or strategic advisory services without becoming an employee or standard vendor:

- Engaging senior industry veterans, domain experts, or academic researchers as company advisors
- Compensating advisors with stock options or equity vesting over time rather than cash fees
- Ensuring all ideas, deliverables, and inventions developed during advisory sessions belong to the company
- Setting strict boundaries around confidentiality and trade secret protection
- Navigating university or institutional employer conflict-of-interest policies

## Why This Template

Created by experienced technology and startup attorneys at General Legal, this template is specifically tailored for tech startups that need to balance attractive advisory equity incentives with airtight IP protection. Key features include:

- Clean stock option grant language compatible with standard startup equity incentive plans
- Comprehensive IP assignment covering all inventions conceived or developed during the engagement
- Explicit handling of Preexisting IP to prevent advisors from encumbering company technology
- CCPA/CPRA and privacy law data protection requirements
- Dedicated institutional policy covenants (crucial for university faculty and researchers)
- Mutual at-will termination plus automatic termination after 12 months of inactivity

## Key Provisions

- **Services**: Description of advisory duties and time commitments with non-compete within the company's specific field
- **Consideration**: Board-approved stock option grant, vesting schedule (typically 1 to 2 years), and pre-approved expense reimbursement
- **Ownership & Inventions**: Comprehensive assignment of all Inventions and work product to the company; Preexisting IP disclosure requirement and perpetual license fallback; moral rights waiver; attorney-in-fact appointment
- **Proprietary Information**: Strict non-disclosure obligations, minimum standard of care, data protection covenants, and federal Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)) immunity disclosures
- **Termination**: At-will termination by either party at any time; automatic termination if no services are requested for 12 consecutive months; survival of IP, confidentiality, and remedies
- **Independent Contractor Status**: Explicit classification as independent contractor with no eligibility for employee benefit plans
- **Promotional Rights**: Authorization for company to use advisor's name, likeness, and biographical information in pitch decks and marketing
- **Institutional Conflict Compliance**: Representation that advisory work does not violate obligations to universities, employers, or research institutions
