## Overview

A comprehensive, multi-jurisdictional Data Processing Addendum (DPA) designed for global software, cloud, and technology companies. It fulfills statutory requirements under the European Union GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), UK GDPR, Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and U.S. state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA, etc.). It incorporates the European Commission's 2021 Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA).

## When You Need This Template

Use this template as an addendum to MSAs or customer agreements when your technology platform processes personal data from users or enterprise employees located worldwide:

- Selling SaaS, cloud platforms, or APIs to multinational corporations or customers operating in Europe, the UK, Switzerland, or the US
- Satisfying mandatory GDPR Article 28 processor obligations
- Legalizing cross-border personal data transfers from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to the US or other third countries via Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- Implementing Module 2 (Controller-to-Processor) and Module 3 (Processor-to-Processor) transfer architectures
- Satisfying US state privacy law requirements alongside European regulations in a single unified contract

## Why This Template

Drafted by specialized international data protection attorneys at General Legal. Cross-border data compliance is fraught with complex regulatory mechanisms (Schrems II requirements, transfer impact assessments, conflicting state and European definitions). This template provides:

- **Complete European Transfer Architecture**: Seamlessly incorporates the 2021 EU SCCs by reference, pre-populating required Annexes and docking clauses without requiring messy separate documents
- **UK & Swiss Adaptations**: Incorporates the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) International Data Transfer Addendum and Swiss FADP modifications
- **Article 28 Alignment**: Complete coverage of mandatory processor duties: technical and organizational security measures (TOMs), audit rights, assistance with data protection impact assessments (DPIAs), and regulatory consultation
- **State Privacy Harmonization**: Combines GDPR terms with US state privacy law provisions (Annex 2) to avoid conflicting dual-addendum nightmares

## Key Provisions

- **Scope & Governance**: Regulates processing of Customer Personal Data across EEA, UK, Switzerland, and US jurisdictions
- **Article 28 Obligations**: Strict confidentiality of personnel, adherence to documented customer instructions, and assistance with data subject rights requests
- **Cross-Border Transfers & SCCs**: Automated application of EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision (EU) 2021/914) Modules 2 & 3, UK IDTA, and Swiss revisions for transfers to third countries without adequacy decisions
- **Security Measures & TOMs**: Commitment to industry-standard technical and organizational measures (encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, incident response)
- **Sub-processor Framework**: General written authorization model with advance notice of changes and reasonable objection rights
- **Security Incident Management**: Timely notice of confirmed personal data breaches to enable timely regulatory notification (e.g. 72-hour GDPR windows)
- **Audit Rights**: Compliance verification via SOC 2 / ISO certifications and third-party audit reports before on-site inspections
- **End of Service Handling**: Secure deletion or return of all personal data upon agreement expiration
