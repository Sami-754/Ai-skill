## Overview

An Employee Offer Letter outlines the core terms, compensation, and conditions of employment for salaried, exempt employees based in California. It establishes an at-will relationship, integrates the Employee Confidential Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (CIIAA), defines flexible PTO/vacation policies, and includes an FAA-governed mandatory arbitration clause with class action waivers.

## When You Need This Template

Use this template when making an employment offer to a full-time, salaried exempt professional, manager, software engineer, or executive working in California or for a California-headquartered startup:

- Extending formal job offers to full-time engineering, product, sales, or managerial candidates
- Specifying base salary, payroll frequency, manager reporting line, and office vs. remote work locations
- Establishing a flexible, non-accruing vacation policy (avoiding costly California accrued PTO cash-out liabilities)
- Protecting company proprietary IP by conditioning employment on execution of a CIIAA
- Enforcing individual binding arbitration under the Federal Arbitration Act with class action waivers

## Why This Template

Drafted specifically for California's strict labor and employment legal landscape. California has some of the most stringent employment regulations in the United States, particularly regarding overtime exemptions, vacation pay accrual, and arbitration enforceability. This template incorporates:

- **California Labor Code Compliance**: Clear classification as exempt from overtime pay under California law
- **Unlimited / Flexible Paid Time Off**: Properly structured non-accrued vacation language to comply with California rules regarding PTO payout upon separation
- **Federal Arbitration Act (FAA) Shield**: Comprehensive individual arbitration clause designed to satisfy California unconscionability standards (company pays arbitration fees, adequate discovery, written reasoned award, preserves court injunctive relief)
- **Prior Employer Protection**: Explicit covenants preventing new hires from bringing or using former employer trade secrets, shielding the company from tortious interference and trade secret misappropriation claims

## Key Provisions

- **Role & Responsibilities**: Job title, core duties, direct supervisor, work location (office vs. remote), and employer discretion to adjust
- **Compensation & Payroll**: Annual base salary, pay frequency (semimonthly / biweekly / monthly), and standard withholdings
- **Benefits & Flexible Vacation**: Health and welfare plan eligibility; non-accruing vacation policy where vacation does not vest or cash out upon termination
- **At-Will Employment**: Explicit mutual at-will employment relationship that cannot be altered except by written officer agreement
- **Confidentiality & Invention Assignment**: Requirement to sign Exhibit A (CIIAA) prior to start date
- **Prior Employer Non-Disclosure**: Representation and agreement not to bring, use, or disclose any third-party or prior employer proprietary information
- **Dispute Resolution & Arbitration**: Mandatory confidential individual arbitration administered by JAMS under the FAA; class/collective action waiver; carveout for PAGA/FEHA non-arbitrable claims as permitted by law
- **Contingencies**: Offer conditioned on satisfactory reference checks and US work authorization (Form I-9)
