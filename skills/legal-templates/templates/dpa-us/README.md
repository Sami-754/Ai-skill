## Overview

A processor-friendly Data Processing Addendum (DPA) tailored for vendors, service providers, and SaaS companies processing personal data subject to United States federal and comprehensive state privacy laws (including California CCPA/CPRA, Virginia VCDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut CTDPA, and Utah UCPA).

## When You Need This Template

Use this template as an addendum to customer MSAs or terms of service when your product or service processes personal data originating from US residents:

- Providing B2B SaaS, cloud infrastructure, or API services to US enterprise customers
- Complying with statutory requirements under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by CPRA) and other state privacy laws
- Establishing provider status as a "Service Provider" or "Processor" rather than a "Third Party" or "Sale/Share" recipient
- Defining security standards, sub-processor engagement rules, incident notification protocols, and data deletion commitments
- Executing an addendum where customers do not process EU, UK, or Swiss personal data (for global data, use `dpa-global`)

## Why This Template

Drafted from a service provider-friendly perspective by General Legal's privacy attorneys. Many customer-drafted DPAs impose onerous, open-ended audit requirements and unbounded liability. This template provides a balanced, compliance-focused framework:

- **State Privacy Law Ready**: Integrates statutory restrictions preventing "selling" or "sharing" personal data, retaining data outside the direct business relationship, or combining personal data across customers
- **Realistic Incident Notification**: Clear definition of Information Security Incidents that excludes standard benign network scans, pings, and failed login attempts
- **Streamlined Sub-Processor Rules**: General authorization model with advance notice of new sub-processors and reasonable customer objection procedures
- **Sensible Audit Provisions**: Enables verification through independent security certifications and documentation before triggering burdensome on-site audits

## Key Provisions

- **Role Clarification**: Customer acts as Business/Controller; Provider acts as Service Provider/Processor
- **Scope & Instructions**: Provider processes data solely in accordance with Customer's documented instructions and the underlying agreement
- **State Privacy Law Annex (Annex 2)**: Mandatory CPRA / state privacy restrictions preventing sale, sharing, or commercial exploitation of personal data
- **Security Measures**: Administrative, technical, and physical safeguards (Annex 3) designed to protect customer personal data
- **Incident Response**: Prompt notification to Customer of confirmed Information Security Incidents with reasonable mitigation assistance
- **Sub-processors**: Customer approval mechanism for existing and new downstream vendors (AWS, cloud providers, database hosting)
- **Data Subject Requests**: Assistance with consumer access, deletion, and opt-out requests under applicable state laws
- **Data Return & Deletion**: Deletion or return of personal data upon contract expiration or written request
